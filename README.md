Museum of the Year 3000 — a single museum label, written by a program. The label a museum in the year 3000 places beside a parking meter from today:

> Parking Meter, c. 2024. Humans paid coins to borrow the ground their vehicle was already standing on.

The curators of 3000 assume the meter rented out the ground itself — the car was already parked on it. `main.ts` writes that one line (101 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, on every run.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
